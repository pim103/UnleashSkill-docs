    Statuses, events, damage¶

    User version: Statuses, Events, Damage and reactions, Dynamic values.

    Principle: nothing gameplay-related is hard-coded. A need is first expressed as configuration data (status, event, category, tag, mark) carried by a generic behavior. A new coded behavior must serve several pieces of data; never a specialized node (no “OnCrit”, but Chance + Mark branch + On event).

    Statuses¶

    • A StatusDefinition (UUID) carries: positive / negative, category, max stacks, duration, stacking policy, presentation, and a polymorphic list of behaviors ([SerializeReference] behaviors).
    • The holder is an IStatusHolder (SkillTarget): its StatusContainer (Apply, Remove, RemoveWhere, AddDuration, AddStacks, Has, GetStacks, IsImmuneTo, BlocksSpell…).
    • StatusStepper (world step listener) advances the durations at the holder's step (clock.DeltaTimeFor(holder)), then StatusBehavior.Step.
    • Application and removal emit the native events Status applied / Status removed (with StatusRemovalReason).
    • Diminishing returns per category (StatusCategory), modulated by the entity's reaction profile (boss).

    Existing behaviors: PeriodicStatusBehavior (damage or heal per stack, through the pipeline), StatModifierStatusBehavior (additive, %, or frozen replacement), LockStatusBehavior, UntargetableStatusBehavior, IntangibleStatusBehavior, ImmunityStatusBehavior, RemoveOnEventStatusBehavior, EventOnlyStatusBehavior, and the interceptions: DamageTakenMultiplierStatusBehavior, AbsorbHitsStatusBehavior, RedirectStatusBehavior, MinimumHealthStatusBehavior, ReflectStatusBehavior.

    Add a status behavior¶

    1. [Serializable] class derived from StatusBehavior in Configuration/ (assembly UnleashSkill, serialized by reference: never rename or move it afterwards).
    2. DisplayName (English text through Tr), public fields with [Tooltip] in English (French in fr.po); a tunable number is a ValueSource (evaluated with the instance: see InterceptionFilter.Evaluate).
    3. Useful hooks: CreateState (state per application), OnApplied, OnStacksChanged, OnReapplied, OnRemoved, Step(instance, state, deltaTime).
    4. The Statuses panel of the main menu offers it automatically (TypeCache.GetTypesDerivedFrom<StatusBehavior> in StatusConfigPanel).
    5. If it acts on damage: call it explicitly at its place in DamagePipeline.Intercept (statuses.ForEach<YourBehavior>(…)). There is no generic hook: the order of the pipeline is a gameplay rule.
    6. EditMode tests (test holder, StatusContainer, SimulationClock.Fixed clock), then a skill that uses it, cast in a test world.

    Damage pipeline¶

    DamagePipeline is the only path for damage: Simple damage, Advanced damage and the ticks of periodic statuses go through it, as a DamageHit (attacker, target, amount, type, source node, branch, source status, critical). Order (Apply):

    1. Amount computed by the damage formula (see below).
    2. Immunities and before-hit listeners (SkillTarget.BeforeHitListener): blocked, nothing more.
    3. Damage taken multiplier (product of the factors).
    4. Absorption: the first status with a charge absorbs the whole hit.
    5. Redirection: each redirection takes its % of what remains.
    6. Reflection: its % set aside for the attacker.
    7. Application to the stats, capped by minimum HP.
    8. After-hit listeners, Hit / Kill events, then death of the target (EntityDeathScope: Death, then cancellation of its skills).
    9. Redirected and reflected shares, as secondary hits (steps 2 to 8, without redirection or reflection: no loop).

    Hit listeners receive an OnHitData (Amount, Origin, DamageType, SpellTagUuids, AffectedStats); a before-hit listener blocks the hit by setting ListenerState.Passed to false. Tag listeners (SkillTarget.BeforeTagApply, OnTagApply) receive an OnTagApplyData (TagToApply), stat changes (EntityStats.StatChanged) a StatChangedData (StatUuid, ValueBefore, ValueAfter, HasMaximumValue, MaximumValue, Origin).

    A new damage node computes its amount with ConfigurationData.GetDamageFormula().Compute(...), builds a DamageHit (amount and marks of the DamageRoll) and calls DamagePipeline.Apply; it never modifies a health stat directly.

    Damage formula¶

    DamageFormula (damageFormula field of the configuration, Damage formula panel) is the only amount calculation, shared by Simple damage, Advanced damage, the ticks of periodic statuses. The amount starts from the node's base (amount, Advanced damage stats, tick amount × stacks), then each declared step (DamageStepDefinition: UUID, name, operation) applies in list order. No step is specific to the code: the operation is generic.

    Operation (DamageStepOperation) Effect
    DamageTypeModifiers DamageType.ApplyModifiers: modifiedByStat on the current amount (attacker's stats; without an attacker, ignored)
    NodeAndBranchMultipliers node multiplier (Advanced damage) × BranchDamageMultiplier
    Add / Multiply adds / multiplies by value (ValueSource)
    Roll roll of chance (%, the execution's randomness); automatically successful if the branch carries markUuid and markForcesSuccess. Success: amount × value, markUuid applied to the hit

    A step with appliesToPeriodic false is skipped by the ticks. The default formula (CreateDefaultSteps): damage type, multipliers, then the Critical, a Roll step like any other (0%, × 2, Critical mark, not on ticks), under the UUID DamageFormula.CriticalStepUuid that the nodes' old internal critical hits map to. A node overrides the chance and value of a step by UUID (DamageStepOverride, damageStepOverrides field).

    The marks of successful rolls are carried by the hit (DamageHit.Marks, DamageEvent.Marks) and added to the marks of the Hit / Kill event: a listener filters by mark (HasMark), whatever it is. A critical hit is read through its mark (HasMark, mark of the Critical step). An effect per damage type goes through a Hit listener filtered by type, or a Damage taken multiplier status.

    Add an operation: a value of DamageStepOperation handled in DamageFormula.Compute and its fields shown by DamageStepDefinitionDrawer; same vocabulary as the calculation phases planned for stats (base = Set, then ordered operations), no second engine.

    Events¶

    • EventDefinition: natives (fixed UUIDs in the code, EventDefinition.Natives, cannot be deleted) and custom (configuration). Natives: event-hit (Hit), event-kill, event-death, event-status-applied, event-status-removed, event-spell-cast, event-cast-interrupted, event-summon-created, event-obstacle-contact. Their names are in English in the code and translated at display time (context event).
    • GameEvent: definition, source, subject, source execution, skill tags, branch marks, value, status and stacks, reason, position.
    • GameEventBus (UnleashSkillManager.World.Events): Subscribe(uuid, handler), Unsubscribe, Emit. Emit first notifies the statuses of the subject and the source (Removal on event), then the subscribers. Depth and nesting are capped (anti-loop).
    • The game emits a custom event with UnleashSkillManager.EmitGameEvent(uuid, source, subject, value) (parry, successful dodge…).
    • Listener in a graph: OnGameEventModel (On event), filters by role (me, allies, enemies, summons), tags, marks; one branch per event, target = subject or source, value in EventValueKey.

    Add a native event¶

    1. UUID constant and Native(...) entry in EventDefinition (English name, description of the source, the subject and the value), and their French translation in fr.po.
    2. Emitter in GameEvents (RaiseXxx, modeled on RaiseSkillCast) called at the right place in the engine. The engine emits, never a specialized node.
    3. If the event has its own filter (status, damage type), add it to the filter of OnGameEventModel.
    4. EditMode tests on the bus (test subscriber) and an On event case in a cast skill.

    Dynamic values and formulas¶

    • ValueSource: numeric field of a node. Modes (ValueSourceMode): fixed, stat (caster or target; current, max, %), execution value (number of targets, last damage, skill variable, stacks removed, charge…), distance, status stacks, event value, formula. Then value × multiplier + add, clamped if requested. DependsOnTarget: the node evaluates it per target. Outside an execution (native regeneration of a stat: CustomStat.RegenerationAmount and RegenerationInterval), EvaluateForHolder(stats, owner) evaluates for the entity that carries the stat: it is caster and target, its stats are read in stats (even without an owner), execution values, event values and distance are 0.
    • ValueFormula: expression parsed once (Parse), evaluated without allocation. Operators + - * / % ^, min, max, clamp, Stat(name[, Caster|Target][, Current|Max|Percent]), Stacks(name[, Target|Caster]), Var(name), Random() (0 to 100 excluded, SkillExecution.Random; 0 outside an execution), native values (Targets, LastDamage, Distance…). Names read without case or accents, except variables. Division by zero or non-finite result: 0. The French vocabulary of earlier versions (Lanceur, Cible, Courante, Pourcent, Hasard, Cibles, DerniersDegats…, et, ou, non) stays accepted as aliases.
    • Conditions: comparators < > <= >= = != (== accepted) then not, and, or, all under + - (grammar: or → and → not → comparison → sum). A comparison is 1 or 0, a non-zero value is true; equality within ValueFormula.EqualityTolerance (SimulationClock.Epsilon); non-associative comparisons (1 < x < 3: positioned error); and / or without short-circuit (postfix form). and, or, not and their aliases are reserved (Var(and) for a variable with this name). Check condition (ConditionalCheckModel, ConditionCheckType.Formula) evaluates its formula per target if DependsOnTarget, once otherwise; a formula text field takes [SkillFormula] (help and errors in the editor, SkillFormulaDrawer).
    • A new native value: a member of ExecutionValueKind, read by ValueSource, and its entry in the Natives table of ValueFormula (English displayed name, pre-1.0 alias → source), which feeds NativeNames and the editor's help.
