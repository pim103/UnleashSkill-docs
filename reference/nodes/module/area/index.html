    Usage¶

    Area: on each activation, the entities whose hurtbox overlaps the shape go out through the “Targets in the area” output; the listeners (OnHit…) receive the detected entities.

    Choose among the entities in the area: connect a Target selection to the “Selection” input. Its filters, its sort (distance measured from the area's center) and its count replace the area's team filter, on each activation: “the 3 most wounded enemies in the area”. The Selection's source is not read: the candidates are always the entities in the area. The listeners keep their own filter.

    [Area: 6 m circle] ──Targets in the area──► [Heal]
        ▲ (Selection)
[Target selection: allies, lowest stat (HP), 3]

    Area or Spell body? The area detects by itself: on each activation (a single one if Tick Rate is 0), all the entities whose hurtbox overlaps its shape, whether it moves or not. The Spell body hits what its body meets on its path, swept by the world on each tick: it is the module of a projectile. For an area (explosion, aura, poison puddle), an Area; for an object that flies and strikes, a Spell body — followed if needed by an Area at the impact point.

    Walls: by default, walls and the scenery do not cut an area. An entity whose hurtbox overlaps the shape is detected, even behind a wall. With Require Line Of Sight, an entity is kept only if nothing cuts the line from the area's center to its own: the static scenery (Scenery Layers) and the bodies of the Obstacle layer (walls, Blocker that blocks the caster's side) hide it, an explosion no longer hits behind a wall. The line goes from the center, not from the edge of the shape: a pillar inside the area hides what stands behind it, seen from the center. In turn-based mode, the same rule applies to the selected entities, from the area's center. The listeners receive the same entities.

    Number of activations: a first activation at launch, then one every Tick Rate seconds, as long as it falls strictly before the end of Duration: for a Duration above 0, ⌈Duration / Tick Rate⌉ activations (Duration 3 and Tick Rate 0.5: 6 activations, at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5 s; none at 3 s). The intervals are regular from the first one, counted in simulation time (time scales and hitstop included) from the tick that detects at launch. A Duration of 0 or a Tick Rate of 0 gives a single activation. In turn-based mode, the same rule in turns of the caster: Duration 3 and Tick Rate 1, 3 activations (at launch, then on its next two turns).

    Listeners: an On hit under the Area applies its Hit Policy to the detected entities: with Rehit Once, an entity is hit once over the area's whole duration; with Every Tick, at each activation; with Cooldown, again once Rehit Cooldown has elapsed in the area's time (a Tick Rate of 0.5 and a Rehit Cooldown of 1: once every two activations). Its Max Targets ends the area once reached: with the default policy (1 target), the area ends at its first entity, and the Node Editor shows the warning W116 on the On hit when the area has a Duration. For an explosion or an aura, set Max Targets to 0.

    Permanent aura: an Area has no unlimited duration (Duration 0 = instant, a single detection). For an aura that lasts as long as its execution (a passive), use a Timer with a Total Duration of 0, followed by an instant Area anchored on the caster, or by a Target selection around the caster.

    [Start] ─► [Timer] (0.1 s, 0, Fire Immediately) ─► [Area] (Anchor: caster, 4 m, Duration 0) ──Targets in the area──► [Simple damage]
