    Damage and reactions¶

    Damage types¶

    A damage type (Physical, Magical, Fire…) is chosen by each damage node and each periodic status. It carries modifiers that adjust the damage based on stats: for example, the target's armor reduces physical damage.

    Modifier setting Role
    Stat Stat read (of the attacker, or of the target with Use the target's stat).
    Modification type How it applies to the damage.
    Value source Fixed value, or stat multiplier.

    Status immunities can target damage types.

    The damage pipeline¶

    All damage (Simple damage, Advanced damage, periodic statuses) goes through the same path, in this order:

    1. the subject's immunities;
    2. damage taken multiplier (status);
    3. absorption (shield that absorbs N hits);
    4. redirection of a share of the damage to another entity;
    5. reflection of a share to the attacker;
    6. application, with an optional minimum HP (“bleeding cannot kill”);
    7. events (Hit, Kill, Death).

    Steps 1 to 6 are status behaviors.

    Reactions¶

    A reaction is what a hit does to the target's body: knockback, stun, freeze. They are applied by the Reactions node.

    Behavior Effect
    Displacement Knockback, pull; outward, lateral, vertical directions, or along the attacker's aim.
    Freeze Target hitstop: its time freezes for a moment.
    Applies a status For example Stun applies the Stunned status.
    No native effect The game or a listener decides.

    A new configuration contains Knockback and Stun.

    Profiles per entity type¶

    A profile adapts the reactions to an entity type: a boss is knocked back less (Multiplier), only beyond a Threshold, or never (Immunity). Per status category, it also sets a duration multiplier (Duration ×), an immunity, and the No diminishing returns option.
